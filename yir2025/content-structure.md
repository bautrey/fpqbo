## SECTION 1: LETTER FROM LEADERSHIP

### Headline
**Stronger Together: A Year of Growth and Partnership**

### Copy
2025 marked another milestone year for Fortium Partners. As we reflect on the past twelve months, we're proud of what our partner network has accomplished together—helping over 200 companies navigate financial challenges, scale operations, and achieve their strategic goals.

Since our founding in 2014, we've built a national network of elite CFO talent, united by a shared commitment to delivering exceptional financial leadership. This year, we welcomed 18 new partners to our family, expanded our geographic reach, and continued to demonstrate that fractional finance leadership isn't just a cost-effective alternative—it's often the superior choice for growing companies.

Thank you to our partners for their dedication, our clients for their trust, and our team for making it all possible.

**Here's to an even stronger 2026.**
